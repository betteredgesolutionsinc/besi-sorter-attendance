        Required versus actual headcount for every shift. Required headcount is set once per week. Only shifts that are lacking or overmanned are flagged.

        The 7, 8 and 9 PM shifts count on the date they punch. The 12:00 AM and 1:00 AM shifts count with the same night, just like the night shifts. Only the 4:00 AM–1:00 PM shift is recorded on the next day.
